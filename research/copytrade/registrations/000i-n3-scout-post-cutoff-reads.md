# 000i — N3 scout: announcement titles and literature after the cutoff

Ledger entry, not a hypothesis. Written 2026-10-12 by the orchestrator, from
the N3 listings scout's report (`research/copytrade/scouts/n3-listings.md`,
flags section). The scout's own scripts were end-dated; these reads came
from two web desk-research helpers it used.

## What happened

1. **Binance announcement catalog (CMS catalog 48), read newest-first.**
   The helper saw article titles and dates after 2026-07-01, including a
   CTUSDT listing notice dated 2026-10-01 and "Pre-IPO perp" titles from
   September 2026, and the catalog total (2,280 articles today). Upbit's
   trade-notice total today (788) was also read. Neither API accepts an end
   date.
2. **Literature published after the cutoff.** The Tie (2026-07-13,
   2026-07-30) and Streamflow (2026-09-02). Streamflow's unlock aggregates
   include The Tie data through 2026-08-19, so aggregate, qualitative
   post-cutoff unlock outcomes were in view.
3. **Public priors on the listing calendar.** Published studies already
   report post-listing returns for the 2023–2026 listings (Animoca 2024,
   Memento 2025, Four Pillars 2025, The Tie 2023-01 → 2026-06). Our own
   computations have not touched those windows; the public's have.

## What was seen

Titles, dates and totals of announcements; qualitative and aggregate
published findings. No price, return or funding series after 2026-07-01 was
fetched or computed by our code.

## What must not follow from it

- The 2026-07 → announcement calendar is **partly seen** and may not serve
  as an unread holdout for any announcement-based rule.
- No N3 parameter (entry delay, hold, stop, strata, sizing) may be set from
  the post-cutoff articles. The direction of the post-listing effect is
  public knowledge and may motivate a hypothesis; its magnitude in a
  published study may inform power only, labelled as such.
- An unlock rule may not use 2026-07 → 2026-08-19 as unseen data.

## Mitigation

- Announcement and unlock lights are not drafted this round (no free
  point-in-time calendar; announcement APIs cannot be end-dated).
- A listing rule states that every decision calendar is fresh to our
  computations but not to public priors, and that only data after
  2026-07-01 is unseen by both.
